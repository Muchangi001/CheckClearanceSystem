Capture a cheque

Outward clearing. Scan both sides, key the MICR line as printed, and key the amount: CTS cheques carry no pre-printed amount.

MICR line

MICR code = city (3) · bank (3) · branch (3). Demo drawee banks: 002 SBI, 240 HDFC, 229 ICICI, 211 Axis.

Instrument
Images

Production CTS sends front grey-scale (100 DPI JPEG) plus front and back black & white (200 DPI TIFF G4). Each image is hashed with SHA-256 here and the hashes are signed at presentment.
